For best results, use a modern phone browser. Sensor access may require HTTPS and permission. If no readings appear, try opening the page in Chrome or Safari on your phone.

How this helps

SmartLevel can help check the approximate tilt of a shelf, tabletop, ramp, or equipment surface. Place the phone steadily on the surface and compare its angle before and after adjustment.

Measurement note: this app reports device orientation angles supplied by the phone/browser. Sensor conventions and accuracy vary by device, and the readings are not a substitute for a calibrated engineering instrument.
